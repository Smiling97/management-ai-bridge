# Contributing to Management AI Bridge

Management AI Bridge welcomes contributions that help management scholars, educators, students, and practitioners use GenAI responsibly and effectively.

## Contribution Types

- **Resource submission:** suggest a course, tool, paper, dataset, or community.
- **Prompt submission:** share a reusable management-focused prompt.
- **Tutorial request:** suggest a workflow that needs a tutorial.
- **Correction request:** report an outdated link, unclear guidance, or factual issue.

## Editorial Principles

Contributions should:

- Focus on management research, teaching, organizing, decision-making, or productivity.
- Translate AI capabilities into practical workflows.
- Include limitations, risks, and review criteria where relevant.
- Avoid uploading confidential, private, copyrighted, or sensitive material.
- Link to large files rather than committing them directly.

## How to Submit

Open a GitHub Issue using the most relevant template in `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/`.

For prompts and tutorials, include enough context for another member to evaluate the use case, required inputs, expected output, and risks.

## Review Process

Maintainers will review submissions for relevance, clarity, responsible AI considerations, and fit with the project structure. Accepted contributions may be edited for style and consistency before publication.
