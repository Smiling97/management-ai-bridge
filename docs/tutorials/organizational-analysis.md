# GenAI for Organizational Analysis

## Purpose

Use GenAI to structure analysis of organizational situations, identify competing interpretations, and prepare better questions for diagnosis.

## When To Use

Use this workflow when analyzing a case, consulting situation, organizational change effort, leadership challenge, or team process.

## Workflow

1. Describe the organizational situation using nonconfidential information.
2. Identify stakeholders, goals, constraints, and visible symptoms.
3. Ask the AI system to generate alternative diagnoses rather than a single explanation.
4. Compare diagnoses against available evidence.
5. Ask what evidence would distinguish between explanations.
6. Convert the analysis into discussion questions or a decision memo.

## Example Prompt

```text
Analyze this organizational situation as a management case.

Situation:
[describe the situation without confidential details]

Known stakeholders:
[list stakeholders]

Visible symptoms:
[list symptoms]

Constraints:
[list constraints]

Generate:
1. Three plausible diagnoses
2. Evidence supporting each diagnosis
3. Evidence that would weaken each diagnosis
4. Missing information to collect
5. Risks if managers act on the wrong diagnosis
6. Questions for a team discussion
```

## Output Review Checklist

- Does the analysis distinguish symptoms from causes?
- Are multiple explanations considered?
- Does the output respect uncertainty?
- Are stakeholder interests represented fairly?
- Are recommendations proportional to the evidence?

## Risks

GenAI may over-attribute behavior to individuals, ignore power dynamics, or recommend generic interventions. Use it to structure thinking, not to replace direct evidence or ethical judgment.
