# GenAI for Literature Review

## Purpose

Use GenAI to organize, compare, and synthesize management literature while keeping source verification and scholarly judgment central.

## When To Use

Use this workflow when starting a new research project, preparing a seminar, mapping a construct, or comparing streams of literature.

## Workflow

1. Define the focal question, construct, population, and management context.
2. Gather verified sources from scholarly databases before asking the AI system to synthesize.
3. Provide abstracts, notes, or citation metadata as input.
4. Ask for themes, tensions, assumptions, methods, and gaps.
5. Check every claim against original sources.
6. Convert useful output into a literature matrix or annotated outline.

## Example Prompt

```text
You are helping with a management literature review. Use only the source notes I provide.

Research focus: [insert topic]
Management context: [insert context]
Source notes:
[paste abstracts or verified notes]

Create a synthesis table with these columns:
1. Study or source
2. Core construct
3. Theoretical lens
4. Method
5. Key finding
6. Boundary condition
7. Relevance to my research question
8. Claims that require verification

After the table, list three unresolved tensions across the sources.
```

## Output Review Checklist

- Are all claims grounded in supplied sources?
- Did the model invent citations, findings, or author names?
- Are theoretical tensions clearly separated from empirical findings?
- Are gaps substantive rather than generic?
- Does the synthesis preserve nuance across methods and contexts?

## Risks

GenAI can hallucinate citations, flatten theoretical differences, overstate consensus, or turn weak patterns into confident claims. Never use AI-generated literature claims without checking the original source.
