# Responsible Academic Use

## Core Practices

- Verify AI-assisted claims against original sources.
- Do not invent or rely on AI-generated citations.
- Follow institutional, journal, classroom, and collaborator policies.
- Avoid uploading confidential data, unpublished interview transcripts, private student work, or restricted materials.
- Disclose AI assistance when required or when it materially shaped the work.

## Research Use

AI can help structure literature reviews, draft code comments, critique arguments, and generate alternative explanations. It should not replace source verification, theory judgment, research ethics, or methodological transparency.

## Teaching Use

AI can help generate discussion questions, examples, rubrics, and assignment drafts. Instructors should review materials for accuracy, inclusiveness, pedagogical fit, and academic integrity implications.
