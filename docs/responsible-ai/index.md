# Responsible AI

Responsible AI use in management requires attention to accuracy, privacy, transparency, bias, power, accountability, and learning.

## Guidance Areas

- [Academic Use]({{ '/responsible-ai/academic-use/' | relative_url }})
- [Organizational Use]({{ '/responsible-ai/organizational-use/' | relative_url }})
- [Limitations and Risks]({{ '/responsible-ai/limitations-and-risks/' | relative_url }})

## Baseline Standard

Use GenAI to support thinking and drafting, not to replace human responsibility. When AI influences research, teaching, or decisions, the user remains accountable for the quality and consequences of the output.
