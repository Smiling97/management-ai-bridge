# Limitations and Risks

## Common Risks

- **Hallucination:** AI may produce false facts, citations, or confident explanations.
- **Privacy exposure:** Users may submit sensitive information to tools that store or reuse data.
- **Bias:** Outputs may reproduce social, organizational, cultural, or historical bias.
- **Overreliance:** Users may accept fluent outputs without enough review.
- **Generic advice:** Outputs may miss context-specific constraints and politics.
- **Hidden value judgments:** Decision support may conceal assumptions behind neutral-sounding language.
- **Accountability gaps:** Teams may be unclear about who is responsible for AI-assisted work.

## Practical Controls

- Use verified source material.
- Ask for assumptions and uncertainty.
- Require review criteria in prompts.
- Keep confidential data out of unapproved systems.
- Document AI use when appropriate.
- Review outputs with domain expertise.
