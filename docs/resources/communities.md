# Communities

This page tracks communities that can help management users learn about AI, share applied practices, and stay connected.

## Community Types

- Management and organization research communities
- AI literacy and education communities
- Responsible AI communities
- Practitioner communities focused on productivity and organizational adoption
- Technical communities useful for understanding AI infrastructure

## Submission Notes

When submitting a community, include the audience, participation model, link, and why it is relevant to management users.
