# Productivity Prompts

## Meeting Notes to Action Plan

### Use Case

Turn rough meeting notes into clear next steps.

### Input Needed

Notes, participants, goals, and deadlines.

### Prompt

```text
Convert these meeting notes into an action plan.

Meeting goal: [goal]
Participants: [participants]
Notes:
[paste notes]

Create: decisions made, open questions, action items, owners, deadlines, risks, and a concise follow-up email.
```

### Expected Output

Structured action items and a draft follow-up.

### Review Criteria

Verify owners, deadlines, and whether the output invents decisions.

## Management Memo Draft

### Use Case

Draft a concise memo from rough analysis.

### Input Needed

Audience, decision context, evidence, recommendation, and constraints.

### Prompt

```text
Draft a concise management memo.

Audience: [audience]
Decision context: [context]
Evidence: [evidence]
Recommendation: [recommendation or open question]
Constraints: [constraints]

Use this structure: situation, recommendation, rationale, risks, next steps, and open questions.
```

### Expected Output

A clear memo draft for review and editing.

### Review Criteria

Check accuracy, tone, omitted caveats, and fit with the audience.
