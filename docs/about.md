# About Management AI Bridge

Management AI Bridge is a community knowledge hub for translating GenAI from technical capability into practical management work.

The project is built for management scholars, educators, students, and practitioners who want clear, responsible, field-relevant guidance. It treats management as an integrated domain rather than dividing content into separate strategy, organizational behavior, entrepreneurship, or management subfields.

## Mission

The mission is to help the management community use AI to improve how people research, teach, analyze, decide, organize, and collaborate.

## What Makes This Different

Many GenAI resources focus on technical depth: models, coding, infrastructure, benchmarks, and deployment. Management AI Bridge focuses on translation:

- What task is the management user trying to complete?
- What information should be provided to the AI system?
- What output is useful?
- How should that output be reviewed?
- What risks, biases, privacy concerns, or quality problems must be managed?

## Intended Audiences

- Management researchers designing studies, reviewing literature, analyzing data, or developing theory.
- Educators preparing classes, cases, assignments, feedback, and assessment materials.
- Students learning how to use AI responsibly for knowledge work.
- Practitioners applying AI to organizational analysis, strategy, decision support, and productivity.
- Community members who want a shared place to exchange tools, prompts, and lessons.

## Why We Established This Project

This project was established to appreciate and share the knowledge we have gained through generous support from NSF NAIRR and related community learning activities.

By organizing workshop materials, tutorials, prompts, and responsible-use guidance in one open space, Management AI Bridge helps the community continue learning together and apply GenAI more thoughtfully in management research, teaching, and practice.
