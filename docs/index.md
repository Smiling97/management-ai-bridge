## Why This Exists

Most AI communities explain models, tools, infrastructure, and code. Management users need a different layer: practical translation from AI capability to management work.

Management AI Bridge focuses on how GenAI changes how people study organizations, teach management, analyze situations, make decisions, coordinate work, and improve productivity.
