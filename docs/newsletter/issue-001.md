# Issue 001: Why Management Needs an AI Bridge

## Opening Note

GenAI is moving quickly, but management users do not only need more tools. They need practical translation: how to use AI responsibly in research, teaching, organizational analysis, decision support, and everyday knowledge work.

## Management-Relevant AI Update

The most important shift is not only model capability. It is workflow redesign. Better management use of AI depends on clearer tasks, better prompts, source verification, privacy judgment, and human review.

## Tool or Workflow of the Month

Start with a literature synthesis workflow. Provide verified source notes and ask for a structured comparison table, then check every claim against the original papers.

## Prompt of the Month

Use the [Literature Synthesis Matrix]({{ '/prompts/research-prompts/#literature-synthesis-matrix' | relative_url }}) prompt to compare sources by construct, theory, method, finding, boundary condition, and relevance.

## Teaching or Research Application

In teaching, GenAI can help instructors generate multiple discussion pathways for a case. The instructor should still choose the flow, adapt examples, and check whether the questions support the learning objectives.

## Responsible AI Reminder

Do not submit private student data, confidential organizational information, unpublished interview transcripts, or sensitive research material to tools that are not approved for that use.
